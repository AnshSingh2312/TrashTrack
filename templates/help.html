Frequently Asked Questions

How do I report littering?

Visit the Incident Report page, enter the necessary details, and upload a photo.

How can I make a payment for a fine?

Go to the My Challans page and click the "Pay" button next to each fine.

What if I receive a fine incorrectly?

If you believe a fine was issued in error, please contact us through the Help Page.
