About Us

Welcome To Our Website

TrashTrack is a Website application designed to promote cleanliness by issuing fines for
throwing dirt or litter on public roads. Our goal is to create cleaner, safer communities by holding
individuals accountable for their actions. Whether you're reporting violations or issuing fines,
TrashTrack makes it easy to enforce proper waste disposal and raise awareness about the importance of a clean environment.
